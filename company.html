Our story

Built to make buying a home feel simple

Meridian Realty started with a simple frustration: by the time most buyers heard about a property, the best ones were already gone. We built a members-first agency to change that.

Since 2011 we've helped thousands of families move first — sharing new listings before they go public, flagging price changes the moment they happen, and pairing every member with a local expert who actually knows the streets they're shopping on.

We're not a listings portal and we're not a call center. We're a small team of local agents who believe good information, shared early and honestly, is what turns a stressful search into a confident decision.

What we stand for

The principles behind every listing

Local expertise

Every agent lives and works in the areas they cover, so advice is grounded in the real market — not a national average.

Radical transparency

Clear pricing, honest estimates, and no pressure. If a property isn't right for you, we'll tell you before you fall for it.

Members first

Our members see listings early and off-market opportunities first. That access is the whole point — and it stays free.
